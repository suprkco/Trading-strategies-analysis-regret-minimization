Evolve strategy DNA, then let online learning decide whom to trust

A genetic algorithm breeds 120 trading strategies, each encoded as 36 genes, on three US indices. The fittest survive, cross over and mutate for 60 generations. Champions are picked on a later validation window, frozen, then tested on a market they never saw, where Hedge and Fixed Share combine them day by day.

No look-aheadEvery signal at day t uses closes up to t−1; costs are charged on every change of exposure. The test index is never loaded while breeding.
Selection, not tuningGenes are weights over eight signals in calm and stressed regimes, look-back periods, bias, gain, dead zone, shorting, volatility target, smoothing, stop-loss and cooldown.
Why combine onlineChampions that looked best in the past can fail. Hedge's regret against the best of them grows sublinearly, whichever one that turns out to be.
