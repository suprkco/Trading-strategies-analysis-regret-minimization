# Five-minute interview walkthrough

1. Open the live page. Let the evolution play: the training score climbs towards 2 while the same genome's validation score stays near zero. Name it: overfitting of a 36-gene search space.
2. Point at the DNA heatmap converging, then at the champions. Explain the two diversity mechanisms and why some champions are weak: decorrelation was imposed, and the learner decides whom to trust.
3. Pick a clean test market (DJ Transportation, Bitcoin). Show the price, the equity curves and the weights moving.
4. Scroll to the seeds x markets heatmap. Say the result plainly: buy & hold wins in a rising 2024-2026 market, but Hedge reliably beats the average champion on three markets and is never reliably worse. That is exactly what the regret guarantee promises.
5. Explain what was declared in advance (markets, costs, score, parameters), and why the Nikkei carries an asterisk.
6. If asked about "real alpha": daily technical signals on public data are a weak edge. The value here is the measurement protocol, not a trading claim. Next steps would be more seeds, volume-aware costs on tradable instruments, and walk-forward re-training.
